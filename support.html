What support includes

  • Answers to questions about Gamut's settings, sections and blocks.
  • Help finding a feature and setting it up.
  • Fixes for bugs in the theme. Fixes are free and come in theme updates.
  • Help updating to a new version of Gamut.

What support doesn't include

  • Changes to the theme's code, or help with code that you or someone else changed.
  • Design work, such as building pages or choosing pictures.
  • Setting up apps, or problems that an app causes. We check whether the theme is involved and tell you what we find.
  • Store settings outside the theme, such as payments, shipping, domains and taxes. Shopify Support helps with these.

For custom work, you can hire a developer from the Shopify Partner directory.

Before you contact us

  • Look for your question in the FAQ.
  • Make sure you use the latest version of Gamut. In Online Store > Themes, the theme shows a notice when an update is available.
  • If you or a developer changed the theme's code, check whether the problem also happens in a fresh copy of Gamut from your theme library.
  • Turn off apps one at a time to see whether one of them causes the problem.

What to include in your message

  • Your store address, for example yourstore.myshopify.com.
  • The style you started from (Gamut, Sprig, Pinwheel or Gearbox) and the theme version. The version is in Online Store > Themes, under the theme's name.
  • The page and the section with the problem.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording.

Changing the theme's code

If you change the theme's code, duplicate the theme first (Online Store > Themes > … > Duplicate) and make your changes on the copy. We can't support changes to the code. Code changes aren't carried over when you update the theme, so keep a note of them.
