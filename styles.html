Styles and the product data they use

All four styles share the same sections and settings, so you can mix their features. A beauty store can use the comparison table, and a toy store can use quantity breaks. The differences are the defaults: colors, fonts, shapes, motion and the sample page layouts.

Several features read product metafields. Create each definition once in Settings > Custom data > Products, then fill it in on each product (or import it with a CSV). Every feature hides itself on products that have no data, so nothing breaks while you fill them in.

Metafields used by the theme

Name Namespace and key Type Used by
Badges custom.badges List of single line text Custom badges on cards and product pages, such as "Vegan" or "Bestseller". Turn on in Theme settings > Badges.
Ingredients custom.ingredients Rich text Collapsible row or tab on the product page
How to use custom.how_to_use Rich text Collapsible row or tab
Specs custom.specs Multi-line text Specs table block and the comparison table (compare from the catalog). One Label: Value per line.

Safety custom.safety Single line text Safety note on the product page

Installation guide custom.install_guide URL Document link block
Condition custom.condition Single line text Structured data (new, refurbished, used)

You can use any metafield as a collection filter in the Search & Discovery app. Concern, skin type, goal, diet, age group, interest and position all make good filters.

Gamut (beauty)

  • Shades: name your variant option "Shade" or "Color". Add a swatch color or picture to each value in the admin, and the theme shows swatches on cards and product pages.
  • Before and after: add the Before and after section, with two photos taken in the same light.
  • Routines: the Shoppable image section pins products onto a photo. The Scrolling story section walks through steps, with a picture for each.

Sprig (supplements and wellness)

Supplement Facts. Add a multi-line custom.supplement_facts metafield, one line per row:

Serving size: 2 capsules
Servings per container: 30
Magnesium (as magnesium glycinate) | 200 mg | 48%
Ashwagandha root extract | 300 mg | †
Other ingredients: Hypromellose capsule, rice flour
  • Lines with | become table rows: name, amount and % daily value.
  • Lines with : are serving facts.
  • "Other ingredients" goes under the table.
  • Write † where no daily value exists.

Other features:

  • Subscriptions: install a subscriptions app, such as Shopify Subscriptions. The product page then shows one-time and subscription options side by side.
  • Quantity breaks: add the Quantity breaks block to the product template and set each tier, for example 2 units for 10% off. For each tier, create a matching automatic discount (Amount off products, minimum quantity) in Discounts. The block shows the saving and checkout applies it. B2B customers see their catalog's own price breaks and quantity rules automatically:
    • the price for the chosen quantity on the product page;
    • "Volume pricing available" on product cards;
    • in the cart, the quantity rule and how many more units reach the next price break.
  • Price per serving: set the unit price on each variant in the admin; it shows on cards and product pages.

Pinwheel (toys and kids)

  • Gift finder: the Product finder section asks up to three questions and leads to a filtered collection. The default questions use:

    • custom.age_group for age (add it as a filter in Search & Discovery);
    • price;
    • product type.

    Each question's choices must match the filter values exactly.

  • Age badges: filled from custom.age_group.

  • Gift wrap: create a product called "Gift wrap" and choose it in Theme settings > Cart > Gift wrap.

Gearbox (auto parts)

Vehicles. In Theme settings > Vehicle finder, list the vehicles you sell parts for, one make and model per line:

Toyota | Camry | 2018-2024 | 2.5L I4, 3.5L V6
Honda | Civic | 2016-2021 | 2.0L I4, 1.5L Turbo I4

For long lists, use a shop metafield custom.vehicles (multi-line text) in the same format.

Fitment comes from product tags:

  • Fits: Toyota / Camry fits every year and engine.
  • Fits: Toyota / Camry / 2019 fits one year.
  • Fits: Toyota / Camry / 2019 / 2.5L I4 fits one engine.
  • Universal fit fits every vehicle.

You can add tags in bulk with the product CSV or bulk editor.

What the store then does:

  • Vehicle finder: customers choose their vehicle once. Every product page then says whether the part fits, and lists all the vehicles it fits.
  • Vehicle pages: each vehicle gets its own collection page, for example /collections/all/fits-toyota-camry, titled "All parts for Toyota Camry".
  • Part numbers: customers can search by SKU or by OE number, from custom.oe_numbers.

Make and model names describe fitment only. Don't use car makers' logos.
