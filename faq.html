Questions and troubleshooting

A section shows products I didn't choose. Until you choose a collection, product or picture for a section, Gamut fills it from your store, so the theme looks like your store from the start. Select the section and choose what you want. The inspector's setup guide counts these sections, and Highlight on the page shows where they are.

The stats, the hero card or the reviews show numbers that aren't mine. That is sample text to show the layout. Replace it with your own figures, or remove the block, before you publish.

Some product blocks don't appear. Blocks such as Supplement Facts, specs, fitment and custom badges hide themselves when a product has no data for them. Add the metafield on the product; see styles and data.

The vehicle finder has no vehicles. Add your list in Theme settings > Vehicle finder, or create the shop metafield custom.vehicles. Then tag your products with Fits: Make / Model tags.

The gift finder shows no results. Every answer must match a filter value in your store. In Search & Discovery, add the filters the questions use, such as the age group metafield. Then make sure each choice in the gift finder is spelled exactly like the filter value.

Filters don't appear on collection pages. Install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app and add filters there. Gamut shows whatever filters you set up.

Subscriptions don't show on the product page. Install a subscriptions app, such as Shopify Subscriptions, and add the product to a subscription plan. The options then appear automatically.

Which customer accounts does Gamut support? Both.

  • New customer accounts (Settings > Customer accounts): customers sign in with a one-time code. Shopify hosts the account pages, and you style them in Settings > Checkout and accounts > Customize. The account icon in the header opens the sign-in and the account menu; choose that menu in the header settings.
  • Classic customer accounts: Gamut includes sign in, create account, account overview, order details, addresses, account activation and password reset pages in the theme's style. Each has its own template in the theme editor (Templates > Customers), with a color scheme setting.

A customer reached a reward in the cart bar but didn't get it at checkout. The rewards bar only shows progress. Create a matching rule in Shopify for each reward, at the same amount:

  • Free shipping: a free shipping rate (Settings > Shipping and delivery) or a free shipping discount.
  • Free gift: an automatic "Buy X get Y" discount.
  • Percentage off: an automatic "Amount off order" discount with a minimum purchase amount.

The bundle builder shows a saving but checkout doesn't apply it. The builder only shows the saving. In Discounts, create an automatic "Amount off products" discount for the same collection, with a minimum quantity equal to the items per bundle and the same percentage.

Animations feel too strong, or I don't want them. Go to Theme settings > Animation and choose Subtle or None. Visitors who turn on "reduce motion" on their device never see them anyway.

The inspector badge is in the way. Only you see it, in the theme editor, and only after you turn it on. Click it to close the panel, or uncheck Theme settings > SEO and structured data > Show page inspector in the theme editor.

I use an SEO app that adds its own structured data. Turn off Theme settings > SEO and structured data > Enhanced structured data. The theme then falls back to Shopify's standard data, so you don't get duplicates.
