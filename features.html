Header, footer, menus and empty pages

  • Mega menu. A menu item with three levels opens a wide panel with a column for each link. To add pictures, open Header in the theme editor and add a Menu promotion block: type the menu item's title and pick a collection. The promotion shows the collection's picture, title and number of products, or your own picture and text. Promotions with nothing chosen show collections from your catalog, so the menu looks finished from the first day.
  • Search window. Before the customer types, it shows the customer's recent searches, your popular searches as buttons and four products. Type the searches in Popular searches in the header settings (separated by commas) and pick a collection in Products to suggest, or leave it empty to show products from your catalog. As the customer types, products show as small cards, four in a row on desktops (two across on tablets, a list on phones), beside collections and pages and articles; articles show their picture and date, and those with the search in their title come first. Search for "…" stays at the bottom of the window. Customers can also open search with Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac).
  • Smart search. Shopify's search matches whole words and completes only the last word typed, so "srum", "barier" or part of a part number ("1042" for BP-1042) find nothing. With Smart matching on (Theme settings > Search), the theme fills those gaps from your 1,000 best-selling products: close matches appear in the search window and on the search page, a misspelled search gets Did you mean "serum"?, and the letters that matched are marked in the titles of products, collections, articles and pages. A search for a part number or barcode opens the exact variant. Shopify's own results always come first.
  • Sold-out products in search. In Theme settings > Search, show them last (the default), in the normal order, or hide them.
  • Search page. Products fill the grid; matching articles and pages follow under Articles and pages, each with its picture (or a page mark), date and a short excerpt.
  • Sticky header. The header gets a soft shadow once the page scrolls under it.
  • Search field in the header (Gearbox uses it). On small desktops, where the menu wouldn't fit beside the field, the logo, field and icons share the first row and the menu gets a row of its own.
  • Transparent header. In the Header settings, choose Over a full-width banner (home page or all pages). When a page starts with a Hero with text over the picture, or a full-width Slideshow, the header sits on the picture with light or dark text, and turns solid when customers scroll, point at it, use the keyboard or open a menu. Add a Logo on the banner, such as a white version of your logo, if your logo is dark.
  • Phone menu. The menu drawer shows your menu promotions as a row of pictures to swipe, a link to the customer account and your social media icons.
  • Contact details in the footer. Add a Contact block to the Footer: a phone number that starts a call on phones, an email address, opening hours, a short note and a button, such as a link to your contact page. Leave a field empty to hide it.
  • Collection pages. With Show collection image on in the Product grid section, the page opens with a panel: the collection's title and description beside its picture. Collections without a picture show the title alone.
  • Grid or list. Turn on Show grid and list view switch in the Product grid section to let customers switch views (Gearbox opens in the list). In the list, each product shows its SKU, brand, rating and compare checkbox on one line under the title, with the add button beside it.
  • Filters on phones. The filter drawer keeps Clear all and Show 12 results at its bottom; the number updates as filters change, and the button closes the drawer onto the results.
  • Cart drawer suggestions. Products from your cart suggestions show as a row of small cards under the cart items, with a round + on the picture when product cards use the icon quick add.
  • Empty cart drawer. Besides the message and Continue shopping, it shows three products from your cart suggestions collection (or your catalog). It follows the cart suggestions setting in Theme settings > Cart.
  • Cart page. On phones the order summary and checkout come right after the products; gift wrap and suggestions follow. The order note opens from a link.
  • Stock limit in the cart. For products that track inventory and stop selling at zero, the quantity in the cart page and cart drawer stops at what's in stock, and the line says Only 4 available. The note also shows when Shopify lowers a quantity on its own.
  • Contact page. Add up to four Contact detail blocks to the Contact form section (email, phone, hours, returns…): they show as small cards with icons above the form.
  • Empty cart, search without results and the 404 page show a short message, the next step (keep shopping, search again, browse all products) and four popular products. Choose the collection, or turn the products off, in the settings of the Cart, Search results and Page not found sections.

Password page and gift card

  • Password page (while the store is closed): your logo, a heading and your password message, an email signup (Notify me) that adds visitors to your customer list, and your social media icons. The password field opens from Enter using password. With Picture beside the text, add a picture; without one, your store name appears large on a soft color. Edit it in the theme editor under Others > Password.
  • Gift card product page: amounts as plain buttons, and I want to send this as a gift opens the recipient's email, name, message and send date (turn it off in the Buy buttons block). Delivery dates, stock, pickup, shipping notes and bulk prices stay off gift cards, since nothing ships.
  • Gift card page: the balance on a card in your button colors, with the code to copy, the QR code, Apple Wallet, printing and a link back to the store.

Home page sections

Banners and stories

  • Hero: split, overlay or stacked. Optional picture motion (settle or parallax) and a floating card for a rating or guarantee. With text over the picture, choose light or dark text and its position (top, center or bottom); the overlay follows the text color and sits behind the text, so the rest of the picture keeps its colors. Add a Mobile image to frame the picture for phones.
  • Slideshow and Image with text. Image with text can shape its picture as an arch, circle, organic blob or leaf, with a circle, dot grid or outline frame behind it in your accent color.
  • Scrolling story: steps on one side; the picture changes as customers scroll.
  • Scrolling text: a moving band of short phrases and small pictures, with a pause button. Three styles: a straight Band, a Tilted ribbon laid across the page, or Crossed ribbons (a quieter second tape in another color scheme runs the other way behind the first, smaller, slower and without pictures). The text moves more slowly on phones. For visitors who prefer reduced motion, the phrases stand still and wrap onto several lines.
  • Numbers: key figures that count up when they come into view.
  • Countdown banner: for a real end date only; it never restarts.

Products

  • Featured collection and Collection tabs. With Mobile layout: Swipe, products swipe in one row on phones, and on tablets too when they would leave a short last row (five products in rows of three).
  • Featured product: a full product form on the home page.
  • Comparison table: products side by side, rows from prices, ratings or metafields. On phones the labels stay in place while the products scroll, and the edge fades while there are more to see.
  • Bundle builder: customers pick a set number of products (2–6) from a collection and add them all to the cart at once. The box shows the saving; create a matching automatic discount (Amount off products, that collection, minimum quantity) so checkout applies it.
  • Shoppable image and Lookbook: products pinned on photos.
  • Bought together, Recently viewed.
  • Quick order list: every variant of one product with its price breaks and a quantity, added to the cart in one step. On phones each variant is a block of its own, with the quantity beside its total. Quantities stop at the stock left, with Only 4 available under the variant.

Navigation

  • Collection list: tiles, arches or circles.
  • Product finder: questions that lead to a filtered collection, such as Pinwheel's gift finder.
  • Vehicle finder: hidden from customers until you add a vehicle list (Theme settings > Vehicle finder); the theme editor shows how.
  • Store locator: each store's address, opening hours, phone, email and a link to the map; empty details are left out.

Trust

  • Testimonials (columns with lines, or cards with a quote mark; each name gets a circle with its initials), Press quotes (a publication's logo, or its name set like a masthead), Logo list.
  • Columns: icons with short texts, plain or in a soft circle or square.
  • Before and after, FAQ (with FAQ structured data). With the stacked layout and no heading, the questions sit in one column right under a page title.

Content

  • Rich text, Video, Blog posts, Email signup, Contact form, Custom Liquid.

Ready-made designs

In Add section, several sections come in more than one design, ready to fill in:

  • Hero, Hero: full-width image and Hero: centered.
  • Image with text, Image with text: arch and Ingredient spotlight.
  • Columns, Icons with text and Steps.
  • Collection list and Collection circles.
  • Rich text and Call to action.

Ready-made pages

Gamut includes page templates with the sections already in place. In the admin, open a page and choose the template under Theme template, or create it in the editor.

Template What's on it
page.about Your page text, your story in an arched picture, your values, key numbers, reviews and a call to action
page.faq Your page text, questions in groups (with FAQ structured data for search engines) and a contact call to action
page.shipping Your page text, shipping, returns and refunds in three steps, shipping questions and a contact call to action
page.lookbook Your page text, a lookbook, a shoppable picture and products to shop
product.story The product page plus a scrolling story, reviews, product questions and recommendations, for products that need more explaining

Pictures and products fill in from your store until you choose your own. Replace the sample text before you publish.

Product page blocks

On desktop, the pictures and the details stay side by side as the page scrolls: whichever column is shorter stays in view until the longer one ends. Turn it off with Keep pictures and details in view while scrolling in the product page section. On phones, the edge of the next picture shows so customers know they can swipe.

Pictures. In the product page section, Media layout sets how the pictures are shown:

  • Grid: all pictures at once, the first one large.
  • Carousel with thumbnails: one large picture with a strip of small ones beside it. The strip is as tall as the picture and scrolls when there are more; the arrows and the picture number sit on the picture. On phones the strip runs under the picture.

Clicking a picture (with Enable image zoom on) opens a full-screen viewer:

  • one picture at a time, with arrows, a swipe, the arrow keys or the small pictures at the bottom;
  • zoom in and out with the + and − buttons or keys, a click (a double click zooms back out) or a double tap, the mouse wheel or a pinch;
  • while zoomed in, drag the picture (or use the arrow keys) to look around; the full-size picture loads only then;
  • Esc, the close button or the browser's back button returns to the page; on phones, drag the picture down.

Each block is a separate piece, so you can reorder, remove or add more of them. Blocks that read product data, such as Supplement Facts and the specs table, show an example in the theme editor until the product has its own data; customers never see the example.

  • Basics: title, price, rating, SKU, badges.
  • Buying: variant picker, quantity breaks, buy buttons, inventory status, delivery estimate, pickup availability.
    • The variant picker shows options as buttons, as cards with each option's price and saving, or as a dropdown.
    • Color options show swatches, swatches with their names, or each variant's own picture.
    • Sold-out options are crossed out.
    • After a product is added, the button shows Added with a check for a moment.
    • When the chosen variant is sold out, customers can leave their email under the button. Turn it on with Offer an email when sold out in the Buy buttons block (off by default). You receive it as a contact form message with the product and variant, so you can write back when it's in stock.
  • Details: description, collapsible rows and tabs (connectable to metafields), size guide, specs table, Supplement Facts, vehicle fitment, document link.
  • More: trust badges, notice, complementary products, share.
  • Gift cards: stock, delivery dates, pickup, complementary products and quantity discounts are hidden on gift card pages. Trust badges are hidden too (Hide for gift cards, on by default), and so is any collapsible row where you check Hide for gift cards, such as Shipping and returns.
  • Story product page (template product.story): steps, customer quotes and FAQ answers you haven't filled in yet show a note in the theme editor only. Customers see each one once it has content.

Blog

  • Blog page: the newest article opens the page large, with its picture beside the text; the others follow in a grid. Choose Grid in the Blog section for equal cards. Tags above the articles filter the list.

  • Article page:

    • reading time and a share button under the title;
    • a thin bar at the top of the screen that fills as the reader scrolls;
    • tags that open the blog filtered by that tag;
    • Keep reading: three more articles from the same blog, those with a shared tag first.

    Each one can be turned off in the Blog post section.

Customer accounts

Works with new customer accounts (hosted by Shopify, opened from the header's account icon) and with classic accounts.

The classic accounts have themed pages:

  • sign in, with password reset on the same page and, beside it, what an account offers (order tracking, buying again, a faster checkout) and Create an account;
  • create account, with an optional email sign-up and the same list of what an account offers (turn the list off with Show account benefits in the Sign in and Create account sections);
  • account overview: tabs for orders, addresses and signing out; each order as a card with its product pictures, status and total, Track shipment once it has shipped, a link to the order and Buy again, which puts the order's items that are still for sale back in the cart; the default address beside it. Before the first order, four products to start with: choose the collection in the Account section;
  • order details: where the order is (placed, preparing, shipped or partly shipped), each shipment once with its carrier, tracking number and Track shipment, then items, discounts, shipping, taxes, refunds and addresses, with Buy again at the top;
  • addresses as cards, where customers add (from a tile after the cards), edit and delete addresses, with a country and state list;
  • account activation and password reset.

Built for every customer

  • Accessibility: keyboard and screen reader support, visible focus, 24-pixel touch targets, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in every style. Visitors who turn on "reduce motion" don't see animations.
  • Speed:
    • no frameworks;
    • scripts for a section load only when it comes near the screen;
    • pictures are sized for each screen.
  • Languages: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish and Dutch, in the store and in the theme editor.
  • Search engines and AI agents:
    • structured data for products, collections, your business, breadcrumbs and FAQs;
    • a cart that shopping agents can update without reloading the page.
