Version history

1.0.0

First release, with four styles: Gamut (beauty), Sprig (supplements and wellness), Pinwheel (toys and kids) and Gearbox (auto parts).

  • 38 sections and 34 blocks you can add, available in every style.
  • Rewards in the cart: free shipping plus up to two more rewards, shown on one progress bar.
  • Bundle builder, compare from the catalog, quick add and quick view.
  • Supplement Facts panel, gift finder with age filters, and vehicle finder with fitment on product pages.
  • Quantity breaks, and B2B price breaks and quantity rules for B2B customers.
  • Ready-made page templates: About, FAQ, Shipping and returns, Lookbook and a longer product page.
  • Ready-made section designs, image shapes, background textures and three primary button styles.
  • Variant picker with cards that show each option's price, swatches with names or variant pictures.
  • Mega menu with collection pictures, friendly empty cart, search and 404 pages with product suggestions, and quick add as an icon on product pictures.
  • Product pictures and details that stay in view side by side, a blog that opens with the newest article large, and articles with reading time, sharing, a reading progress bar and more articles to read.
  • A phone menu with collection pictures, a filter drawer that shows the number of results, a secure checkout line with payment icons, and contact details on the contact page.
  • A search window that suggests popular searches and products before typing, and collection pages that open with the collection picture.
  • A password page with an email signup and a picture, and a gift card page that shows the card itself.
  • Testimonials as cards with initials, and bundle builder cards whose options and buttons line up.
  • A back-in-stock email request on sold-out variants, and product suggestions in the empty cart drawer.
  • Smart search: finds products from part of a word, a part number fragment or a misspelling, suggests the corrected search, marks the matched letters, opens the variant a part number belongs to, remembers recent searches and opens with Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac).
  • Product suggestions in the cart follow the quick add style: a round + when product cards use the icon.
  • A transparent header over a full-width banner or slideshow, with its own logo.
  • Product cards line up row by row (a title on two lines no longer pushes the details below it), with compact ratings and one badge on small phone cards.
  • Reward labels in the cart sit under their markers, collection promo tiles show the whole picture, and articles and pages read in one centred column.
  • Order history as cards with product pictures and a Buy again button; larger press quotes with publication mastheads; footer columns that share the width evenly.
  • Account pages with tabs, products to start with before the first order and address cards with a tile to add one; a sign-in page that shows what an account offers; a footer Contact block with a phone number, email and opening hours.
  • Order pages that show where the order is (placed, preparing, shipped) with each shipment's tracking, a tracking link on shipped orders in the account, and tabs that fade at the edge when there are more to scroll.
  • Banners with light or dark text over the picture, at the top, center or bottom, with an overlay that stays behind the text; cart drawer suggestions as a row of cards; a comparison table whose labels stay put on phones.
  • Tablet layouts: four products or collections share one row, and image with text and scrolling story steps sit side by side; the mega menu opens below a transparent header; on phones the back-to-top link steps aside for the sticky buy bar.
  • Small desktops (990–1199 px): with the search field in the header, the menu gets its own row instead of wrapping; the vehicle finder puts its text above the fields; products and posts that would leave a short last row swipe in one row instead (with the Swipe layout).
  • Search page: products first, then articles and pages in a list of their own; checked for text that doesn't fit in all six languages.
  • Collection list view: the part number (SKU), brand, rating and compare sit on one line under the title, with the add button in its own column; every section checked in every color scheme of the four styles for contrast.
  • Search window: products as cards four in a row on desktops, articles with their picture and date, matched letters marked in every title and Search for always in view; on phones, the quick order list shows each variant as a block with its quantity and total instead of a table to scroll sideways.
  • Right after installing: the banner shows three of your products side by side (or a wide collection picture), and promo tiles show your products whole instead of placeholders; the page inspector also checks text contrast over background textures; a vehicle finder without a vehicle list stays hidden from customers.
  • Gift card product pages: amounts as buttons, a recipient form that asks for the email before adding, the send date written out in the cart and order, and no delivery, stock, pickup or bulk-price lines; trust badges and rows such as Shipping and returns can be hidden on gift cards (Hide for gift cards).
  • Forms after sending: the field to fix is outlined with the reason under it, the summary sits in a panel at the top and is read out, an address form with errors opens by itself, comment errors use the form's own labels, and "can't add more" shows right under Add to cart and clears when the quantity changes.
  • Softer dark color schemes in all four styles: graphite, cocoa, forest green and navy instead of near-black, with off-white text at about 10–11:1 contrast instead of 13–16:1.
  • Windows high contrast mode: the selected option is outlined in the system highlight color, unavailable options are struck through, swatches keep their colors, and the price slider, free shipping bar and reward steps stay visible; the chosen tab, account page, gallery thumbnail and grid or list view are marked too.
  • Cart quantities stop at the stock left, with Only 4 available under the line on the cart page and in the cart drawer; on the narrowest phones the cart page shows each line's total under its quantity, and the footer phone number shrinks to fit its column.
  • FAQ pages: the questions open and close in one column under the page title, lined up with the page text; the quick order list stops at the stock left too.
  • Setup guide and page inspector in the theme editor (off until you turn it on in Theme settings > SEO and structured data), with example data in blocks that read product details.
  • Product pages on tablets (750–989 px): the pictures and the details sit side by side; a split banner's picture takes under half the screen, so its button shows without scrolling.
  • Sections you add from Add section start with neutral example text that suits any store; the story product page shows unfilled steps, quotes and answers only in the theme editor.
  • Rich structured data for search engines.
  • Works with new and classic customer accounts.
  • English, French, German, Italian, Spanish and Dutch.
  • Pinwheel in raspberry: a milky page with deep berry text, raspberry buttons and badges, blush, mint and berry sections, softer headings and raised buttons; scrolling text as a tilted ribbon or two crossed ribbons; a scrolling strip of thumbnails beside the product picture, and a full-screen viewer that slides between pictures and zooms in and out.
  • Pinwheel after a study of 17 toy stores: white product cards, a butter-yellow section, flat buttons and a darker sale price; slimmer ribbons with a quieter back tape, stickers and an always-visible pause button; the picture number on the product picture; a viewer that loads full-size pictures only when zooming and closes with the back button or by dragging down.
